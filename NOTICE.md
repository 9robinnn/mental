# MENTAL

A firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1, tuned for mental tekno: a live groovebox with sub kicks, acid and
dark drones. Personal work in progress by 9robinnn (9EME).

It is a derivative of **SLOOP** by isod89 (https://github.com/isod89/sloop-fm1, GPL-3.0-only),
which builds on **Felucca** by Leo Kuroshita / Hügelton Instruments. See LICENSE, LICENSING.md and
README.md (SLOOP's own) for the full credits and licences. This tree stays under GPL-3.0-only.

No commercial sample pack is distributed here: user samples are loaded onto the device with the
web editor and stay with their owner.

Installing a custom firmware is at your own risk.
