# MENTAL

A personal firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1, for mental tekno. Private work in progress.

It is a derivative of **HYP-1** by RustyPawz (https://github.com/RustyPawz/fm1-HYP1, GPL-3.0-only,
commit 655ecd1c76f23614cb6cf174ede666d22909eb41), which itself builds on AMB-1 / Felucca by Leo Kuroshita
(Hügelton Instruments) and on X0X by Charles Vestal. See LICENSE, LICENSING.md and README.md (HYP-1's own) for
the full credits and licences. This tree stays under GPL-3.0-only.

Installing a custom firmware is at your own risk.
